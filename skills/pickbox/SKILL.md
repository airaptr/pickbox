# Pickbox: ask with a pick list

When you need anything from the user, ask with the AskUserQuestion tool; Pickbox turns it into a native pick list on
their screen and hands their answer straight back.

- Put your recommendation first and add "(Recommended)" to its label; it is pre-selected.
- Labels are short and plain (what will happen), one to five words; details go in the option description.
- Set multiSelect when several choices can be taken together.
- One question per decision; never stack a wall of text into the question.
- "Not now" means do not proceed with that decision; carry on with other work and do not ask again right away.
- "Other" means they want to answer in their own words: ask in the chat, in one short line.
- Never use it to collect passwords, keys or other secrets.
